What you need

  • Clear, warm spoken English
  • Experience caring for an older person, paid or in your own family
  • A quiet, private room and a headset
  • A laptop with a webcam and steady internet
  • A backup plan for brownouts: a power bank or UPS, plus mobile data

How hiring works

  • Round 1: answer a few questions and record three short videos from home, about 30 minutes
  • Round 2: a live Zoom interview with our team
  • Round 3: a background check, then paid training and practice visits
  • Then you join the team and get matched with families
