How it works

Three steps, then it simply happens every weekday.

We send the HomeTogether TV device

It plugs into the TV your parent already has. The visits come right to their screen.

You pick a time window

Like 7 to 8 AM and 7 to 8 PM. We give you the exact visit time, and it stays the same every day. If a medicine has to be at a set time, tell us and we'll hold it.

Their caregiver visits, you get a note

Monday through Friday. A short note comes to you after each visit.

What 15 minutes looks like

A friendly visit with pill time built in.

8:00 The call rings on her TV. "Good morning, Ruth!"
8:02 How did you sleep? Did you eat breakfast?
8:04 Pill time. Her caregiver stays on while Ruth takes them herself.
8:07 Real conversation. The grandkids, the garden, the ballgame.
8:15 Goodbye until tonight. Your note is on its way.

Our story

Two sisters, one idea.

Samantha opened Caring Companions in Missouri in 2017. Katlin worked with her for several years, in marketing and care coordination. She got to know our families, our caregivers, and how much a good daily visit matters to someone living on their own.

Then Katlin moved to Vietnam, and we found a way to keep working together. Today she leads our caregiver team in Vietnam and the Philippines, and Samantha runs Caring Companions here at home.

We hope to fill a need we hear about every week: families who love their parents dearly, and are worn out from being the 8 o'clock call.

Our caregivers

Caregivers who want to make their day brighter.

Your parent's visits come from the Caring Companions family. Some of our caregivers work with our home care agency in Missouri, and soon in Oklahoma. Others are part of our caregiver team in Vietnam and the Philippines, led by Katlin, our co-owner. Katlin lives in Vietnam and knows every caregiver on her team personally.

Every one of them is kind, patient, and experienced with older adults, and they chose this work because they want to make a difference. Before anyone visits your family, they pass a recorded interview, a live interview with our team, a background check, training, and practice visits. Visits are recorded for quality assurance.
